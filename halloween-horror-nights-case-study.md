# Halloween Horror Nights — Case Study Copy
> Apple-style presentation structure. One idea per slide. Large text. Minimal copy. Self-explanatory without narration.

---

## SLIDE 1 — Title

**Translating Terror**
**Into Ticket Sales**

UI/UX Art Director · Universal Orlando · 2018–2019

---

## SLIDE 2 — Setup

**Halloween Horror Nights is Universal Orlando's biggest annual event —**
**the World's Best Halloween Event.**

Mobile traffic was growing fast. The site wasn't ready for it.

---

## SLIDE 3 — The Problem

**Seven ticket options. One confused guest.**

Complex navigation buried what guests were actually looking for —
on a site that wasn't built mobile-first in the first place.

---

## SLIDE 4 — Business Challenge

**Keep the immersive horror atmosphere.**
**Drive ticket conversions anyway.**

Those two goals aren't usually asked to coexist.

---

## SLIDE 5 — What Was Actually Broken

- **Mobile-First Imperative** — fast-growing mobile traffic on a site that wasn't optimized for it
- **Too Many Choices** — 7 ticket options overwhelmed users before they could decide
- **Cross-Channel Consistency** — digital needed to match advertising and marketing, requiring close collaboration with Creative and Art Directors

---

## SLIDE 6 — The Approach

**Simplify the path. Amplify the fear.**

Mobile-first. Simplified content, CTAs, ticketing, and navigation —
a more direct path to buy tickets without losing the atmosphere.

---

## SLIDE 7 — The Solution

**From 7 options to 2.**

Tickets, Extras, and Vacation Packages split into
their own dedicated pages — so guests stayed focused
on what they came for.

---

## SLIDE 8 — Solution Features

- **Mobile-First Design** — responsive, without sacrificing atmosphere
- **Simplified Choices** — 7 ticket options down to 2
- **Focused Pages** — separate paths for Tickets, Extras, and Vacation Packages

---

## SLIDE 9 — My Role

**UI/UX Art Director, lead designer.**

Worked with the design lead and creative leadership to build
the new responsive site. Partnered closely with the Creative
and Art Directors running advertising and marketing for
a consistent brand experience across every touchpoint.

---

## SLIDE 10 — Impact

**Record-breaking results.**

- Broke attendance records
- Double-digit growth in online ticket sales
- Increased mobile traffic and mobile sales

---

## SLIDE 11 — What I Learned

**Sometimes the best design decision is removing options, not adding them.**

Simplifying choices and focusing user journeys created
a better experience *and* drove real business results —

**proof that user-centric design and conversion
optimization aren't at odds.**

---

## SLIDE 12 — Closing

# Fear sells.

## But only if the guest can find the ticket
## before they lose interest.
