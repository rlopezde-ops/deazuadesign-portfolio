# Next-Gen Portal — Case Study Copy
> Apple-style presentation structure. One idea per slide. Large text. Minimal copy. Self-explanatory without narration.

---

## SLIDE 1 — Title

**Rebuilding the Operating System**
**for 680+ Repair Stores**

Sr. Manager, Product Design · Asurion · 2022–2025

---

## SLIDE 2 — Setup

**uBreakiFix technicians relied on one system for everything.**

Check-in. Diagnosis. Repairs. Payments. Inventory.

It had been built over a decade.

---

## SLIDE 3 — The Problem

**A decade of band-aids doesn't scale.**

Slow. Buggy. Siloed from Asurion's other systems.
New technicians needed 2 weeks just to learn it.

---

## SLIDE 4 — Business Challenge

**We weren't asked to redesign a feature.**

We were asked to replace a system with a decade
of operational maturity — in under two years —
without recreating its complexity.

---

## SLIDE 5 — The Three Cracks

**What was actually broken**

- **Slow & Buggy** — outdated tech stack, unreliable day to day
- **Siloed** — not integrated with Asurion's inventory or supply chain, forcing manual workarounds
- **Unlearnable** — nothing guided technicians; every action had to be memorized

---

## SLIDE 6 — Research

**I went to the stores.**

Interviewed technicians, owners, and customers —
not just at the start, but throughout the project.

---

## SLIDE 7 — Research Activities

**What we did**

- In-store visits and interviews
- Service blueprinting with product, ops, and engineering
- Workshops with technicians, owners, and executives from both Asurion and uBreakiFix
- Continuous prototype sharing, not big reveals

---

## SLIDE 8 — Design Principles

**Five principles that replaced a decade of tribal knowledge.**

- **Adaptable and Scalable** — ramp new programs fast
- **Guided and Helpful** — the system teaches, not the tenure
- **Simple and Efficient** — fewer clicks, fewer steps
- **Smart and Proactive** — anticipate the need before it's asked
- **Reliable** — trusted enough that technicians stop thinking about it

---

## SLIDE 9 — The Solution

**A new platform, one source of truth.**

Built on Microsoft D365 for parts, pricing, availability, and ordering.
Powered by Asurion's design system for consistency and faster dev cycles.

---

## SLIDE 10 — Feature: Customer Experience

**Faster answers for the person standing at the counter.**

Instant price estimates.
Pay by Link for upsells and deposits over the phone.
Search that actually finds what technicians need.

---

## SLIDE 11 — Feature: Built to Onboard Partners

**Days instead of months.**

The old system took roughly two months to onboard a new partner.
The new platform onboards clients like Amazon, Google,
and programs like AT&T in-store pickup — in about a week.

---

## SLIDE 12 — My Role

**Design lead, 0 to 1.**

Built and managed a team of 4 designers.
Set the vision, the principles, and the culture of open critique.
Pushed for customer-facing-level craft on an internal tool.

---

## SLIDE 13 — How We Worked

**Staying aligned across a multi-year effort.**

Weekly design reviews with the full team.
Scheduled ride-alongs and monthly check-ins with technicians and field partners.
Every designer's work tied back to the larger vision.

---

## SLIDE 14 — The Leadership Challenge

**The hardest part wasn't the design.**

Product and operations wanted to rebuild
what stores already had — feature for feature.

Understandable instinct. Wrong move.

---

## SLIDE 15 — Pushing Back

**Respectfully. Persistently. With evidence.**

Recreating a decade of accumulated complexity
wasn't going to fix what technicians dealt with daily.

The design principles became our shared language
for that argument.

---

## SLIDE 16 — Engineering Friction

**Waterfall met iteration.**

Engineering expected fully resolved designs before writing code.
I pushed for shipping something real, learning from technicians
in actual stores, and adjusting from there.

**By launch, design had moved from a service function
to a strategic partner — consulted on roadmap and exec decisions.**

---

## SLIDE 17 — Impact

**Now the operating system for 680+ stores.**

- **680+** stores nationwide
- **50%** reduction in training time
- **1 week** to onboard new partners, down from 2 months

---

## SLIDE 18 — What I'd Do Differently

**Ship to learn, not to feel safe.**

I wish we'd shipped faster and been less risk averse.
The caution that came with replacing a mature, decade-old system
was understandable — but it slowed our learning.

**The best design teams aren't the ones that get it right
the first time. They're the ones that learn the fastest.**

---

## SLIDE 19 — Closing

# Next-Gen Portal wasn't a redesign.

## It was proof that design earns a seat
## at the strategy table by showing its work —
## in real stores, with real technicians, over and over.
