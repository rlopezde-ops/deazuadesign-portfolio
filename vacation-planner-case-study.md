# Club Wyndham Vacation Planner — Case Study Copy
> Apple-style presentation structure. One idea per slide. Large text. Minimal copy. Self-explanatory without narration.

---

## SLIDE 1 — Title

**Turning 220+ Resorts**
**Into One Right Answer**

Design Manager · Travel + Leisure Co · 2020–2022

---

## SLIDE 2 — Setup

**Club Wyndham had over 220 resorts.**

Across the US, the Caribbean, and Hawaii.

---

## SLIDE 3 — The Problem

**More options created more paralysis, not less.**

Finding the right resort was time-consuming and overwhelming.
No personalization. Social media only pushed the same
popular destinations everyone already knew.

---

## SLIDE 4 — What Was Actually Broken

- **Destination Discoverability** — map and list views were slow, and featured resorts weren't customized to the user
- **Product Education Gap** — prospects from social or email weren't converting; new members didn't know where to start
- **Inventory Balance** — popular resorts were hard to book while comparable resorts sat under-visible with open availability

---

## SLIDE 5 — Who We're Helping

**Members and prospects, at every stage.**

> **Prospect:** "I'm interested in a Club Wyndham membership. What kind of vacations can I go on?"

> **Seasoned Member:** "We love our beach resort. It'd be great to find a similar style resort in Orlando within our budget."

> **New Member:** "I don't know where to start. What resort is right for me?"

---

## SLIDE 6 — The Solution

**Explore Resorts.**

A quiz-style flow that asks the right questions
to understand what vacation someone actually wants.

---

## SLIDE 7 — Two Different Goals, Two Different Flows

**Members get action. Prospects get inspiration.**

Member results lead straight to booking —
while encouraging them to keep exploring.

Prospect results are aspirational, building excitement
and understanding, with a clear path to learn about membership.

---

## SLIDE 8 — My Role

**Design Manager.**

Led the design team across the Club Wyndham website —
owner dashboard, booking flow, and the Vacation Planner.

Managed 2 designers, reviewed our external agency's work,
and partnered with user testing using high-fidelity prototypes.

---

## SLIDE 9 — What I Learned

**Personalization isn't a nice-to-have.**

The same content, presented differently based on user goals,
turned a tedious search into an exciting discovery.

**That's what transforms outcomes.**

---

## SLIDE 10 — Closing

## The right five questions turn it into
## a single, confident answer.
