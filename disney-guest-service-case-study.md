# Disney Guest Service Suite — Case Study Copy
> Apple-style presentation structure. One idea per slide. Large text. Minimal copy. Self-explanatory without narration.

---

## SLIDE 1 — Title

**One App to Replace 60+**

Lead Product Designer · Disney Parks & Experiences · 2019–2020

---

## SLIDE 2 — Setup

**Cast Members had every tool they needed.**

They just needed 60 of them at once.

---

## SLIDE 3 — The Problem

**Guests were frustrated. So were the people trying to help them.**

Multiple apps, paper documents, and manual processes
to assist guests pre-arrival, on-site, and post-visit.

---

## SLIDE 4 — What Was Actually Broken

- **Not Responsive** — 60%+ of in-park staff had only mobile or tablet access, but the tools weren't built for it
- **Too Many Steps** — incident reports required 11 dropdown selections, some with hundreds of options
- **Limited Information** — staff relied on paper flyers and the guest's own app for a clear picture
- **60+ Disconnected Apps** — jumping between systems just to see guest info and take action

---

## SLIDE 5 — Who We're Helping

**Cast Members across every touchpoint.**

In-park merchandise coordinators. Call center specialists.
Guest experience managers. All Disney parks.

---

## SLIDE 6 — Guiding Principles

**Inform. Simplify. Empower.**

Give staff the guest and park information they need.
Give them one view instead of many.
Let more staff assist guests in real time, on any device.

---

## SLIDE 7 — The Solution

**Four sections. One responsive app.**

Guest Interaction Tracking. Guest Itinerary & Scheduling.
Tickets & Media. Admin.

Consolidated from dozens of legacy applications —
working seamlessly from desktop to mobile.

---

## SLIDE 8 — Feature: Guest Interaction History

**My design lead area.**

Cast Members log new interactions and review guest history
at a glance, with the ability to act immediately.

The interface adapts to how Cast actually works —
chronological view or grouped by incident.

---

## SLIDE 9 — Feature: MagicBands & Media

**Fast, reliable, in the moment.**

Managing, scanning, and issuing media and tickets —
built in close collaboration with development for quick data transfers.

---

## SLIDE 10 — Feature: Simplified Data Entry

**From 11 dropdowns to a flow that makes sense.**

We redesigned incident reporting to capture guest experiences —
good and bad — correctly and efficiently.

---

## SLIDE 11 — My Role

**Lead product designer.**

Led design on Guest Interaction Tracking.
Conducted research with front-line, guest services, and call center staff.
Grew the design team from 2 to 4.

---

## SLIDE 12 — Impact

**Built collaboratively, adopted enthusiastically.**

- **~25%** efficiency improvement
- **30,000+** staff across 5 parks
- **80%** positive Cast Member response

---

## SLIDE 13 — What I Learned

**Build with your users, not just for them.**

The app's success came from involving Cast Members
throughout — in research, testing, and working sessions.

**When you do that, adoption comes naturally.**

---

## SLIDE 14 — Closing

# Sixty apps became one.

## Not because we added technology —
## because we finally asked the people using it
## what they actually needed.
